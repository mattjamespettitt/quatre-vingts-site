Privacy Policy

Quatre-Vingts: French Numbers · Effective October 2026

In short: the app doesn't collect any data about you. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Nothing you answer leaves your device.

What stays on your device

The app keeps your answers (the number, whether you were right and how long you took), your arcade scores and your settings on your iPhone or iPad, so it can show your progress and bring up the numbers you find hardest. They are never sent to us or anyone else. You can delete them at any time in Settings → Reset progress, and they're deleted when you delete the app.

Speech

Numbers are spoken by the voices built into your device. Nothing you hear or type is sent anywhere, and the app doesn't use the microphone.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple ID, payment details or address. Apple only tells the app whether the unlock has been bought on your Apple ID (or by your family, with Family Sharing), and that check happens on your device. Apple's handling of purchases is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

App Store statistics

Apple gives developers anonymous, combined statistics such as the number of downloads and, if you chose to share them with developers in your device's settings, crash reports and usage figures. These don't identify you.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. Questions: matt.pettitt@gmail.com.
