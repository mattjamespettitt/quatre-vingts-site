Quatre-Vingts: French Numbers

For iPhone and iPad

French numbers are famously tricky: seventy is soixante-dix, eighty is quatre-vingts, four twenties. Quatre-Vingts trains you to read them, hear them and write them until they come instantly, then lets you race the clock in the Arcade.

Everything up to 100 is free. A one-time purchase unlocks every number, all eight arcade levels and Insights. No account, no subscription and no ads, and it works offline.
