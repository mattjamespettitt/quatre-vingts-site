The voice sounds robotic

The app uses the voices built into your device. For a more natural voice, download an Enhanced or Premium French voice: on iPhone or iPad go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French. Then choose it in the app under Settings → Voice. You can also slow the voice down there, or tap Slower during a question.

I can't hear anything

Check the volume and that your device isn't connected to Bluetooth headphones you're not wearing. The app plays sound even with the silent switch on.

Is "vingt-et-un" or "vingt et un" right?

Both. The 1990 spelling reform joins all the parts of a number with hyphens, and both spellings are accepted everywhere in the app. You can choose which one answers are shown in under Settings → French numbers. Belgian and Swiss numbers (septante, huitante, nonante) can be switched on there too.

How do I open the next arcade level?

Each track (written and spoken) has its own eight levels. Pass a level to open the next: reach level 5 in Level up (you go up a level every 5 right answers) or get 18 of 20 in Sprint.

What's free and what's in the unlock?

Everything up to 100 is free: all three practice modes, the first four arcade levels on both tracks, and your progress grid, mix-ups and personal bests. The one-time unlock adds hundreds, thousands, years and any range you like, arcade levels 5 to 8, and Insights (getting faster, practice calendar, best time of day, eyes and ears). There's no subscription.

Restoring your purchase

The unlock belongs to your Apple ID and is shared with your family through Family Sharing, so it works on all your devices. If the app shows as locked after reinstalling or on a new device, go to Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase.

Refunds

Purchases are made through Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
